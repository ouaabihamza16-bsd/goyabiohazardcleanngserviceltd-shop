About us

Specialist cleaning for spaces that need more than a normal clean

Goya Biohazard is the name we trade under as GOYA BIOHAZARD CLEANNG SERVICE LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 16337081) and incorporated on 24 March 2025. We are based in Manchester, and our registered activity is the treatment and disposal of hazardous waste.

We clean and decontaminate rooms and properties affected by biological and hazardous contamination: blood and other bodily fluids, discarded needles and sharps, heavily soiled spaces, and areas that need a thorough disinfection.

These situations are rarely ordinary, and they are often upsetting. Whether it is a family home after a difficult event, a rented property between tenants or a workplace that needs a careful sanitise, we aim to work calmly and discreetly, explain what we are doing in plain words, and hand the space back clean and sanitised.

Services

What we do

Four core services, all focused on making a contaminated space clean and sanitised again, and on handling the contaminated material carefully.

Biohazard & bodily-fluid clean-up

Removal of blood and other bodily fluids from floors, walls, furniture and fixtures, followed by cleaning and disinfection of every affected surface.

  • Blood and bodily-fluid removal
  • Cleaning of hard and soft surfaces
  • Disinfection of the whole affected area
  • Contaminated materials bagged for disposal

Trauma & specialist cleaning

Discreet, compassionate clean-up after traumatic incidents and unattended deaths, and for properties that have become heavily soiled. We treat the space, and the people connected to it, with respect.

  • Clean-up after traumatic incidents
  • Unattended-death cleaning
  • Heavily soiled property cleans
  • A quiet, low-key way of working

Sharps & hazardous waste removal

Safe collection of discarded needles, syringes and contaminated items from homes, gardens, shared areas and workplaces. Items are placed in suitable containers and sent on for proper disposal.

  • Needle and sharps sweeps
  • Collection of contaminated items
  • Safe packaging in suitable containers
  • Waste sent on for proper disposal

Disinfection & infection control

Sanitising homes and workplaces to help reduce the risk of infection, with close attention to high-touch points and shared areas.

  • Whole-room and targeted disinfection
  • High-touch surface sanitising
  • Kitchens, bathrooms and washrooms
  • Homes, offices, shops and shared spaces

Our approach

Why choose Goya Biohazard

Biohazard work is about more than cleaning products. It calls for care, patience and respect for the people involved.

Discreet

We keep a low profile and treat every job, and every detail of it, as private.

Thorough

We clean beyond what is visible, then disinfect every affected surface before we finish.

Respectful

We work calmly and with compassion, especially in homes where something difficult has happened.

Plain-spoken

We explain what needs doing and what we will do, without jargon or pressure.

How a job works

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    You tell us what has happened and where. We listen and ask only what we need to know.

  2. 2

    Assess and protect

    We look at the affected area, contain it and put on protective equipment before starting.

  3. 3

    Clean and disinfect

    Contamination is removed, then every affected surface is cleaned and disinfected.

  4. 4

    Remove the waste

    Contaminated materials and sharps are packaged carefully and sent on for proper disposal.

  5. 5

    Hand back

    We walk you through what was done and return the space clean and sanitised.
